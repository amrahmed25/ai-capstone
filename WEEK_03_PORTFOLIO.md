## 3. Visual Identity Kit

### Typography

**Primary Font:** Inter
**Secondary Font:** Inter

Using one font family with different weights keeps the portfolio consistent and clean.

### Color Palette

* **Primary / Accent:** `#14B8A6`
* **Main Text:** `#0F172A`
* **Background:** `#F8FAFC`
* **Secondary Background:** `#FFFFFF`

### Visual Direction

Clean, calm, and technical.

The design should use near-black text on a near-white background, with teal reserved for important interactive elements and highlights. Generous spacing and simple layouts should keep the focus on the projects rather than the portfolio design itself.

### Logo / Favicon

Use **AM** as a simple monogram for the logo and favicon.

The mark should remain minimal and recognizable without unnecessary gradients, effects, or decorative elements.

## 4. Image Curation

The portfolio should primarily use real screenshots of my projects because the projects themselves are the proof of my skills.

### Final Image Set

* **ElectroHub:** Real desktop screenshot
* **Atlas Web:** Real desktop screenshot
* **Auto Arena:** Real desktop screenshot
* **Meshwar:** Real desktop screenshot
* **Blood Link:** Real desktop screenshot
* **Smart Auto Hub:** Real desktop screenshot
* **About:** Real professional photo
* **Hero:** No generated image; use whitespace and typography

### Image Selection Rule

I will prefer real screenshots of my work over AI-generated images whenever the image is supposed to prove the quality of the project.

AI-generated images can be used only when they support the visual identity without distracting from the actual work.

### Rejected AI Image

**Rejected:** A generated abstract 3D/glass hero image.

**Reason:** It looked generic and distracted from the actual projects. A simple hero with strong typography and whitespace keeps the focus on my work and makes the portfolio feel more intentional.

---

## 5. Design Principle

The portfolio design should frame the work, not compete with it.

The visual system should stay consistent across pages while allowing the actual project screenshots to provide most of the visual variety.

The goal is for a visitor to understand who I am, what I build, and how to explore my work without being distracted by unnecessary visual effects.

---

## 6. Final Direction

**Style:** Clean, modern, technical, minimal

**Typography:** Inter

**Primary accent:** Teal

**Layout:** Spacious, responsive, and content-focused

**Image strategy:** Real project screenshots first

**Main goal:** Make the projects the strongest visual proof while keeping the portfolio itself simple and professional.
