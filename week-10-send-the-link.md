## My Plan for Adding the Next Case Study

My next case study will be added to the Projects/Case Studies section of my personal portfolio.

I will follow the same three-part structure used in Week 2:

### 1. The Problem

I will explain the problem the project solves, who the project is for, and why the solution is useful.

### 2. What I Did
